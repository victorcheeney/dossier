# Dossier glossary

A local app where a Candidate prepares job applications with their own Agent. Each term has one meaning.

## Language

**Candidate**:
The person preparing their Applications with Dossier and their Agent.
_Avoid_: user, person, applicant.

**Profile**:
The Candidate's background, shared by all their Applications: their career history, accomplishments, base résumé, writing voice and job criteria, kept as a few named documents.
_Avoid_: context, memory, bio.

**Profile document**:
One named part of a Profile, such as career history or voice. A Candidate can have any number, with any names.
_Avoid_: section, file, entry.

**Base résumé**:
The Profile document every new Application's Résumé starts from. It is never sent as is.
_Avoid_: master résumé, template.

**Criteria**:
The Profile document saying what the Candidate requires from a job, such as pay, location and hours. The Agent checks each Posting against it.
_Avoid_: requirements, preferences, wishlist.

**Posting**:
An employer's published description of one open job, as the Candidate's Agent captured it.
_Avoid_: job, listing, job ad.

**Application**:
Everything prepared for one Posting: its Answers, its Résumé and its Highlights.
_Avoid_: submission, candidacy.

**Field**:
One question on a Posting's application form, as the employer asks it.
_Avoid_: input, question as a record name.

**Answer**:
What the Candidate will enter in one Field.
_Avoid_: response, value.

**Résumé**:
The document sent with one Application, tailored to its Posting.
_Avoid_: CV.

**Highlight**:
A passage of a Posting the Candidate marked because it matters to them, with an optional note.
_Avoid_: annotation, bookmark.

**Summary**:
The Agent's short view of one Posting, short enough to read without scrolling.
_Avoid_: overview, TL;DR.

**Research**:
What the Agent found about an employer beyond the Posting, such as reviews and questions to ask.
_Avoid_: notes, intel.

**Stage**:
Where an Application stands: Preparing, Applied, Interviewing, Offer or Closed. A Closed Application records why: rejected, withdrew or accepted.
_Avoid_: status, state, pipeline step.

**Note**:
A dated entry on an Application, written by the Candidate or the Agent, saying what happened and where it came from, such as an interview invite read in the Candidate's email.
_Avoid_: comment, log, activity.

**Agent**:
The AI chat the Candidate already uses, such as Claude or Codex, connected to the app. It gathers Postings and drafts Answers and Résumés; the app fetches nothing itself except the Fields of a public Lever, Greenhouse or Ashby form.
_Avoid_: assistant, bot, the AI.

**Example**:
The made-up Candidate, Profile and Posting that ship with Dossier, so anyone can try it before adding their own.
_Avoid_: demo, sample data, fixture.
