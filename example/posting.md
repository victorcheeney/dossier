Product Engineer
Acme · Remote, Canada · Full-time

About Acme

Acme builds tools for small grocers.

Independent grocery stores run on thin margins and long days. Most of them still order stock by phone, count shelves on paper and find out a product is losing money weeks after the fact. Acme gives them one place to order from their suppliers, see what is selling, and price it with confidence. More than 1,800 stores across Canada use Acme every morning before they open.

We are a team of 42, fully remote across Canadian time zones, and profitable since 2024. We grow from what our customers pay us, not from the next funding round.

The role

You will ship features end to end. As a Product Engineer on the Ordering team, you will talk to grocers, decide with your team what to build, write the code across our web app and API, and watch how it gets used once it is live. Nobody hands you a ticket with the answer already in it.

In your first six months you might:

- Rebuild how a grocer places a weekly order, so it takes ten minutes instead of an hour.
- Add supplier price changes to the ordering screen, so a store sees a cost increase before it buys.
- Pair with our support team for a week and fix the three problems grocers call about most.
- Visit a store with a teammate and watch an owner use Acme at 6 a.m.

What we are looking for

- Four or more years building web products that people pay for, with real ownership of what you shipped.
- Strong TypeScript, and comfort with a relational database (we use PostgreSQL).
- You talk to users, and you can tell a request from the problem behind it.
- You write clearly. Most of our work happens in written proposals and pull request descriptions.
- You care about the boring parts: migrations, error messages, tests that catch real bugs.

Nice to have: experience with retail, inventory or logistics software; you have worked a till or stocked shelves.

How we work

- Small teams of four to six, each owning one part of the product, from the first conversation with a grocer to the support ticket after launch.
- Written first. We decide in short documents, and keep meetings for the hard conversations.
- Core hours of 11:00 to 15:00 Eastern; the rest of your day is yours.
- We ship to production many times a day, behind flags when it matters.
- Twice a year the whole company meets in person for a week.

Pay and benefits

- Salary: CA$230,000 to CA$260,000, depending on experience. We share the range for every role and we pay the same within a level, wherever in Canada you live.
- Stock options in a profitable company.
- Four weeks of vacation, plus the week between Christmas and New Year's.
- Health and dental from your first day, and a CA$1,500 yearly budget for your home office and learning.

Our values

- Grocers first. If it does not help a store owner, it can wait.
- Own the outcome. Shipping is the middle of the job, not the end.
- Say it plainly. Clear writing beats clever writing.
- Leave it better. Fix the small thing you walked past.

Hiring process

1. A short application with two written questions. We read every one.
2. A 30-minute call with the hiring manager.
3. A paid take-home: a small feature in a copy of our codebase, about four hours.
4. A conversation about your take-home with two engineers.
5. A chat with a grocer who uses Acme, and with our CEO.

We aim to finish within three weeks and we tell everyone where they stand. You must be able to work from Canada.
