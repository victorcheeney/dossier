# Stories

Stories for interview questions, as situation, task, action, result.

## Booking that patients finish (Fernhill, 2023)

- **Situation:** Half the patients who started booking online gave up before the end. Clinics were taking those bookings by phone instead.
- **Task:** I owned online booking and was asked to find out why.
- **Action:** I watched 20 session replays and called five clinic owners. The form asked for insurance details before showing open times. I moved the times first, asked for insurance after, and shipped it behind a flag to 10% of clinics.
- **Result:** Completion went from 48% to 71% over three months, and we turned it on for every clinic.

## The billing job that kept failing (Quayside Freight, 2020)

- **Situation:** Nightly billing failed about twice a month, and someone had to rerun it by hand before customers saw wrong invoices.
- **Task:** I volunteered to fix it, since I was on call for it anyway.
- **Action:** I split the job into one task per carrier on a queue, so one bad record no longer stopped the rest, and added retries and an alert.
- **Result:** In the next year it failed twice, both retried on their own. Nobody reran billing by hand again.

## A disagreement about scope (Fernhill, 2024)

- **Situation:** My product manager wanted reminder texts, emails and calls in one release.
- **Task:** I thought texts alone would show whether reminders worked at all.
- **Action:** I wrote a one-page proposal with what each channel would cost us to build and support, and asked six clinic owners which they would use. Five said texts.
- **Result:** We shipped texts first, in four weeks. No-shows fell by about a third, and emails came later for the clinics that asked.
