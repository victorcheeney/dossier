# Voice

How I write, for Answers and anything else sent in my name.

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Plain words: "use", not "leverage"; "help", not "empower".
- Numbers only when I can say what they mean.
- First person, active voice. "I built", not "was responsible for building".
- No exclamation marks, no "passionate", no "excited to".
- End when the point is made. No summary line.

## A sample I like

> At Pinecrest I watched a store manager spend Sunday night copying last week's order into a new spreadsheet. I built her a page that started from last week's order instead. She got her Sunday nights back, and that is still the work I like best.
