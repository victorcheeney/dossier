# Accomplishments

## Fernhill

- Rebuilt online booking in 2023. Patients who start booking now finish 71% of the time, up from 48%, measured over the three months before and after.
- Cut no-shows for clinics that turned on reminder texts by about a third. I designed the reminder rules with six clinic owners and built them with one other engineer.
- Wrote the proposal that moved the Scheduling team from a monthly release to shipping daily behind flags. Adopted by all three product teams in 2024.
- Mentor two junior engineers; both now lead features on their own.

## Quayside Freight

- Built the driver app from scratch. Drivers at 140 carriers used it daily by 2021, and it replaced paper delivery slips for most of them.
- Moved nightly billing to a queue. Billing used to fail about twice a month and need a manual rerun; after the move it failed twice in a year, both caught and retried on their own.
- Cut the dispatch screen's load time from 9 seconds to under 2 for our largest customer by fixing three slow queries and paginating the map.

## Pinecrest Market

- Replaced the weekly ordering spreadsheets with a web app. Ordering for a store went from about three hours a week to about one, by the managers' own count.
- Found that the stores were losing money on 40 products after a supplier's price change and flagged them to the owners, who repriced them.
