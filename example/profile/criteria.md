# Criteria

## Must have

- Remote from Ottawa, or hybrid in Ottawa. No relocation.
- At least CA$140K base salary.
- A product people pay for, with customers I can talk to.
- Four-day weeks are a bonus, not a must.

## What I want next

- A product engineer role: talk to users, decide what to build, ship it, see what changes.
- Small businesses as customers. I do my best work when I can picture the person using it.
- A team that writes things down.

## Red flags

- On-call more than one week in six.
- No pay range in the posting.
- "Fast-paced" with no word about how they decide what to build.
