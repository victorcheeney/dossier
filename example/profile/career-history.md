# Career history

I'm Alex Martin, a product engineer in Ottawa. I have built web products for nine years, mostly for small businesses that run on thin margins: clinics, freight carriers and a grocery chain.

## Fernhill · Senior Software Engineer · Remote · 2022 to now

Fernhill makes booking and billing software for independent physiotherapy and massage clinics, about 2,400 of them across Canada. I'm one of five engineers on the Scheduling team.

- I own online booking: the page patients use to book, and the rules clinics set for it.
- I lead our work with clinic owners: I run about four customer calls a month and write up what we learn for the team.
- Stack: TypeScript, React, Node, PostgreSQL, on AWS, with PostHog for session replays and feature flags.

## Quayside Freight · Software Engineer · Montréal · 2018 to 2022

Quayside sells shipment tracking to small trucking companies, from 3 to 60 trucks.

- I built the driver's mobile web app and the dispatch screen it reports to.
- I moved our nightly billing job from a single cron script to a queue, and took on-call for it.
- Stack: TypeScript, Vue, Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL.

## Pinecrest Market · Developer · Ottawa · 2016 to 2018

Pinecrest is a family-owned chain of four grocery stores. I was its only developer.

- I replaced the stores' ordering spreadsheets with a small internal web app for weekly supplier orders.
- I kept the point-of-sale exports and the website running.

## Before that

- Cashier and stock clerk at Pinecrest Market through high school and university, 2010 to 2016.
- B.Sc. in Computer Science, Riverbend University, 2016.
